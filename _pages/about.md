I am a PhD candidate in economics at the University of California, Riverside, advised by Marcelle Chauvet. My research is in international finance and applied econometrics: I study when the relationship between exchange rates and interest rates changes, and what central-bank policy has to do with it. I expect to finish in Spring 2028.

I am looking for industry roles in quantitative research, analytics and economic consulting.

Before the PhD I worked on market sizing at EIU Canback in Cambridge, MA, and on sector research and client forecasting at ICSSAI, a research affiliate of Fudan University in Shanghai. At UC Riverside I have been instructor of record for introductory microeconomics twice, including a 530-student lecture, and I taught the deep learning unit of the department's machine learning course.

I hold an M.S. in economics from Tufts University and B.A. degrees in economics and in Russian from Peking University. I work in English, Chinese, Japanese and Russian.
