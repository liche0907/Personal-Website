## Job Market Paper

**Currency-Specific Breaks and Foreign Exchange Puzzles**  
<span class="entry-meta">Draft in preparation</span>

Textbook theory says a currency should strengthen when its central bank raises interest rates. In the data the link often has the wrong sign. I estimate break dates separately for each of 45 G10 currency pairs over 45 years and re-test the classic exchange-rate puzzles within regimes. In 25 pairs the link moves to the textbook sign at a regime shift, and it never moves the other way. The shifts line up with central banks ending the use of interest rates to defend their exchange rate, a reading supported by daily event studies around policy decisions at four central banks.
