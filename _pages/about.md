I am a PhD candidate in economics at the University of California, Riverside, advised by Marcelle Chauvet. I expect to finish in Spring 2028.

My research is in international finance and applied econometrics. I study when the relationship between exchange rates and interest rates changes, and what central-bank policy has to do with it.

Before the PhD I worked on market sizing at EIU Canback in Cambridge, MA, and on sector research and client forecasting at ICSSAI in Shanghai. At UC Riverside I have twice been instructor of record for introductory microeconomics, including a 530-student lecture.

I am looking for industry roles in quantitative research, analytics and economic consulting.
