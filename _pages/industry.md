## Applied Work

**ICSSAI, Shanghai** (research affiliate of Fudan University's Center for Complex Decision Analysis)  
*Economics & Statistics Analyst / Consultant, Summer 2018 – Summer 2019*

* **Machinery-sector research report.** Built the quantitative part of the firm's report on the machinery industry: fixed-effects panel regressions of listed firms' profitability, solvency and growth, and regression-based forecasts of firm sales and rankings.
* **FDI drivers.** Built a cross-country model of what drives foreign direct investment, for overseas-expansion analysis: fixed- and random-effects panels and regression discontinuity at institutional thresholds. Produced ARIMA demand forecasts.
* **Brexit scenario study.** Forecast sterling and UK real-economy outcomes for a commissioned study in May–June 2019. Sterling was priced by scenario: a level shift for each scenario, scaled by the affected sectors' share of UK trade. The real-economy side used a VAR.
* **Charts and framework.** Co-developed the firm's framework for industry and investment analysis and its house chart style, with a reusable visualization pipeline in R (plotly, ggplot2).

**EIU Canback, Cambridge, MA** (consulting arm of the Economist Intelligence Unit)  
*Business Analyst Intern, Winter 2017 – Spring 2018*

* Handled the data for a market-sizing engagement for a Fortune Global 500 beverage company: sourced, cleaned and screened prefecture-level demographic data used to estimate demand in Chinese cities the client had not yet entered, and ran supporting regressions in Excel and Stata.
* Contributed to the client deck and attended client briefings. Invited to return for a later engagement.

**National School of Development, Peking University**  
*Research Assistant, Spring 2014 – Spring 2015*

* Coded the career histories of national leaders in 50 countries into a structured dataset. The coding scheme underlies the panel in Shi, Xi & Yao (2018).
* Conducted door-to-door surveys of about fifty e-commerce operators in Baigou, Hebei, as one of roughly twenty enumerators.
